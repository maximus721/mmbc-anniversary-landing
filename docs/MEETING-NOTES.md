## 11. Discrepancies I found in the source material

These are real conflicts in the material I was given, not nitpicks:

1. **The anniversary flyer says "SUNDAY, OCTOBER 4TH" with no year.** The Family Fun Day flyer
   says "Saturday, October 3, 2026." In 2026, Oct 3 is a Saturday and Oct 4 is a Sunday, so the
   dates are internally consistent — but the anniversary flyer should carry the year for anyone
   seeing it out of context. Cheap fix, worth doing before it's printed at scale.

2. **The Sunday event price is missing everywhere** — flyer, transcript, and the pricing tiers.
   See §2.

3. **Two venues, two addresses.** Saturday is Walker Park (50 May Ave). Sunday is the church
   (24 Pleasant St). Anyone skimming will mix these up. The site keeps them visually separated
   for exactly that reason.

4. **"Family reunion" vs "Family Fun Day."** The announcement script says "picnic style family
   reunion"; the flyer says "Family Fun Day." Pick one name and use it everywhere, or people
   will think they're two different things.
