Volunteer

We need hands

A weekend this size runs on people who show up early and stay late. Here’s where we need you.

  1. FridayEvening

    Decorating & prep

    Ironing tablecloths and chair covers, and getting the downstairs ready. Honest work, good company.

  2. Saturday10:00 AM

    Park setup

    Meet at Walker Park to set up games, tables and equipment before the noon start.

  3. Saturday4:00 PM

    Park cleanup

    About an hour. Everyone gets a trash bag for their own spot — this crew gathers and hauls.

  4. SundayBefore 1:00 PM

    Celebration setup

    Turning the auditorium around ahead of the one o’clock celebration. It’s a tight window after morning worship, so we need plenty of hands.

  5. SundayAfternoon

    Close-out

    Breaking down after the celebration. Last shift of the weekend, and the most appreciated.

Volunteering doesn’t cover your ticket — we just need the help, and we’re grateful for it.
